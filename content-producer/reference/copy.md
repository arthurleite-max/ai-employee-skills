# Copy

## Voice

Read the brand kit's voice and the sample posts before writing. Match: sentence length, how the owner
opens and closes, emoji use, formality, words they use for their product, words they never use. If
there are no samples, write plainly: short sentences, "you", concrete nouns, no hype.

## Captions

1. **First line** works alone (most people see only it): the hook or the most useful fact.
2. **Body**: short lines, one idea per line, specific details (times, quantities, steps) from the brief.
3. **CTA**: one action (save, share, comment a word, message, book, visit).
4. **Hashtags**: per the brand kit (default 3–5 relevant).
5. **Alt text** for each image: what is in it, in one or two sentences.

Platform notes: Instagram/Facebook caption up to ~2,200 characters but the first line decides; TikTok
short; LinkedIn first two lines before "see more"; X under 280 characters.

## Carousel text

Slide 1: the hook (≤ 8 words, big). Value slides: one point each, title ≤ 6 words + 1–2 lines.
Last slide: the CTA. Keep the same structure across slides so it reads as a set.

## Facts

Every number, price, date, result and claim in the copy must be in the brief or brand kit. Customer
quotes only if real, given by the owner, with permission. Never write a testimonial.
