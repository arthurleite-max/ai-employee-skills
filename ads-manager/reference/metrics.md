# Metrics (always show the division)

| Metric | Formula | Example (fictional) |
|---|---|---|
| CTR | clicks ÷ impressions | 180 ÷ 12,000 = 1.50% |
| CPC | spend ÷ clicks | 270 ÷ 180 = 1.50 |
| CPL (cost per lead) | spend ÷ leads | 270 ÷ 6 = 45.00 |
| Frequency | impressions ÷ reach | 12,000 ÷ 4,000 = 3.0 |
| Cost per real customer | spend ÷ customers in the CRM from that campaign | 900 ÷ 5 = 180.00 |
| Change % | (new − old) ÷ old | (0.9 − 1.5) ÷ 1.5 = −40% |
| Pace | month spend ÷ days elapsed × days in month | 1,500 ÷ 12 × 30 = 3,750 |

Notes
- Leads and conversions come from the platform; customers come from the owner's CRM or sales system.
- Never add Meta and Google conversions together as "customers"; they often count the same person.
- With fewer than about 10 leads, rates jump around: say "early signal" instead of a verdict.
- Frequency applies to social ads (reach is known); search ads have no frequency.
