# Setup: the first conversation

Goal: in about 10 minutes, a brief the whole skill can be built from. Talk to the owner in their language;
accept "skip".

1. Say what you can do here: write every file (any chat), create the folder, audit and zip (where code
   runs), research and test as well (agents).
2. Questions, in short blocks:
   1. The job: what should the skill do, for whom, how often? An example of a real day.
   2. Who will use it and on what AI (free chat apps, paid ones, coding agents)? Their language?
   3. The 3–5 promises, in the user's words.
   4. What must never happen (money, privacy, health, reputation, sending without approval).
   5. Inputs (what the user gives) and outputs (what they get back, in what format).
   6. Anything known that already does part of it (tools, skills, your own process documents).
   7. Name, licence and where it will be published.
3. Write the brief (`templates/brief.example.md`) and wait for the owner's yes before building.
