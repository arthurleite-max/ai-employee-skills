# Writing each slide

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Headline | A full sentence with the takeaway, 14 words or fewer |
| Body | 40 words or fewer; up to 5 bullets; parallel wording |
| Visual | Chart from real data, the owner's own photos, simple icons or a diagram |
| Source | Under every number: where it came from and when |
| Notes | 60–120 words: what to say, the maths behind each number, transitions |

Markers
- `[source needed]`: a fact the owner must confirm or source before presenting.
- `[owner to confirm]`: a price, date, commitment or name only the owner can decide.

Numbers
- Show derived numbers with their maths in the notes: (940 − 800) ÷ 800 = +17.5%.
- Round on the slide, keep the exact value in the notes.
- Same unit and period when comparing.
- The headline must hold for the whole period it describes: the best month is not "per month".
