# Post packs: scripts, carousels, captions

## Reel / TikTok / Short script

```
REEL · 30–35 s · pillar: educate · hook option chosen: B
0–2 s   HOOK   spoken: "You're storing your bread wrong."   on screen: "Stop doing this 👇"
2–10 s  POINT 1 ...  B-roll: bread in plastic bag on the counter
10–20 s POINT 2 ...  B-roll: wrapping in cloth
20–28 s POINT 3 ...
28–33 s CTA    spoken: "Save this for your next loaf."  on screen: "Save it"
Cover text: "Bread storage: 3 rules"
Sound: pick a calm sound from the app's library (low, under the voice)
Shots to film: 6 (list) · Time to film: ~15 min
```

Keep it to what the owner can film in one go. Spoken lines in their voice, short sentences.

## Carousel

Slide 1 hook (big text) · slides 2–8 one idea each (title + 1–2 lines) · last slide CTA ("Save",
"Send to a friend who…", "Order via link in bio"). Design: brand colours and font, high contrast,
readable on a phone.

## Caption

1. **First line** works alone (it is all most people see).
2. **Body**: short lines, the value or the story.
3. **CTA**: one action that fits the goal (save, share, comment a word, message, order).
4. **Hashtags**: 3–8 relevant ones (niche + local + topic), or the profile's policy. No banned or
   unrelated tags.
5. **Alt text** for images (accessibility).
6. Tags and mentions only of accounts the owner approves.

## Check before posting

List anything that needs the owner's decision: health or money claims, prices, people on camera
without confirmed consent, a giveaway (rules and disclosure), a paid partnership (disclosure), a
mention of another business.
