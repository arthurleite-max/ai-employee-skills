# Channel audit (the owner's channel)

## Health in three numbers

1. **Median views** of the last 10–20 long-form videos (the typical result).
2. **Recent median**: last 4–6 uploads. Below the median → declining; above → growing.
3. **Best video** and how many times the median it got.

## What to look for

- **A hit never followed up**: the cheapest opportunity. A second video on the same problem, a deeper
  version, the next question viewers asked in the comments.
- **A format that gets fewer views every time it returns**: keep what works, change the promise.
- **Packaging problems** (with Studio data): low click-through rate with normal retention → title and
  thumbnail; normal CTR and low retention → the first 30 seconds do not confirm the click.
- **Topic drift**: videos that do not serve the audience that subscribed.
- **Link to the business**: does each video lead somewhere (store, WhatsApp, booking)?

## Output

```
Diagnosis: median 1,800 views; last 5 uploads median 1,050 (−42%). Best video "How to unclog a sink
without chemicals" 14,500 views (8.1× the median), never followed up.
Works: problem → solution in under 8 min, object in the thumbnail.
Holds back: generic titles ("Tips for your home #12"); 3 of the last 5 videos are product unboxings.
Next test: a follow-up to the hit.
```

Never treat a single video as the strategy; say how many videos the median uses.
