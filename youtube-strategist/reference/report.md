# The weekly report

Compute everything first; write the headline last, copying numbers from the analysis. One page, in
this order:

1. **Headline**: the week's main finding in one line, with a number.
2. **Your channel**: median vs recent, best video and ratio, the hit not followed up.
3. **What works in the niche**: 3 outliers (title · channel · views · channel median · ratio · date)
   and what they share.
4. **Three topics with room**: topic and angle · score · evidence · fit · risk.
5. **Packaging for the recommended topic**: 10 titles (with character count and issues), top 3
   marked; 10 thumbnail ideas, each paired with a title.
6. **Next video plan** in five lines.
7. **Not followed**: requests or notes you did not follow, with the reason and a truthful alternative.
8. **Not checked**: what the data did not cover (CTR, retention, search volume, videos not provided).
9. **One question**: which topic and which pair to test.
10. **Memory** block (levels 1–2).

Writing rules: every number with its source and date; exact numbers from the computation; "views" not
"views count of about"; no "$" in chat text.
