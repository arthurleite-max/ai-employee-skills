# Attribution without fooling yourself

Every ad platform counts the conversions it touched, with its own window (e.g. 7-day click, 1-day
view). The same customer can be claimed by Google, Meta and the newsletter at once. Adding them up
almost always gives more sales than the business made.

## Rules

1. **Total results come from the source of truth** (sales system, CRM, bank): that is the real number.
2. **Platform numbers are each platform's view.** Show them side by side with the real total:

```
Real sign-ups (CRM): 29
Meta says: 22 · Google says: 19 · sum 41 → 12 more than happened (overlap)
```

3. **Channel split**: use the CRM's lead source (or UTM / "how did you hear about us") for the split;
   if that is missing, say the split is uncertain and recommend fixing it.
4. **Cost per real sale per channel** = channel spend ÷ sales the CRM attributes to that channel.
5. **Tests beat models**: to know if a channel adds sales, change one thing for a defined time
   (e.g. pause one campaign in one region for two weeks) — only with the owner's yes.
6. Organic, referral and repeat customers often carry more than they get credit for: look at them.
