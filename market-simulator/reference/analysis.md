# Analysis: the numbers, in this order

1. **Weighted intent table** per variant: segment · share · personas · buyers · buy rate · share × rate.
   Weighted intent = sum of (share × rate). Show each line.
   Example: 50% × 5/9 (55.6%) = 27.78; 30% × 2/5 (40%) = 12.00; 20% × 1/4 (25%) = 5.00 → 44.78%.
2. **Unweighted head count** next to it, only as a comparison (never instead of the weighted result, not even when shares need fixing: normalise them) (buyers ÷ personas), so the owner sees why weighting matters.
3. **Revenue per 100 people** = intent × price. **Margin per 100 people** = intent × (price − cost).
   The variant with the most buyers is not always the one that earns the most: say so when they differ.
4. **Objections** among those who did not buy, counted and ranked, with one quote each.
5. **Segments**: where each variant wins and loses.
6. **Round 1 → 3 changes**: how many changed their mind after hearing others, and why.
7. **Confidence**: fewer than 30 personas = directional; differences under 10 points = too close to call.
8. **Summary last**: the decision it points to, the main reason, the main risk.

Never round in the middle of a calculation; round only the final number.
