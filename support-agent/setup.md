# Setup: the first conversation

Goal: in about 10 minutes, a profile and a first knowledge base. Talk to the owner in their language;
accept "skip".

1. Say what you can do here: reply drafts and triage (any chat), a sorted queue from an inbox export (where
   code runs), a connected inbox (agents).
2. Questions, in short blocks:
   1. Business, channels (email, chat, social messages), hours, how fast you want to answer.
   2. Your voice: two or three replies you were happy with; sign-off; words to avoid.
   3. Policies: shipping, returns and refunds, cancellations, payments, warranty (paste them or the page).
   4. The 10 questions customers ask most, with your usual answers.
   5. Who takes over red cases, and how to reach them (outside the customer thread).
   6. Identity check before sharing order details (for example order number + email on the order).
   7. Autopilot: keep it off, or list the green topics that may be sent without approval.
3. Write the profile and the knowledge base (one `## Topic` per answer, dated) and show them for approval.
4. Save them (support folder at level 3; code blocks to keep at levels 1–2).
