# Ad policies (summary; check each platform's current rules)

Avoid, and offer a compliant rewrite:
- **Unprovable or health claims**: "lose 10 kg in 30 days", "cure", "guaranteed results", "earn X a
  month". Rewrite around the experience and real facts ("small classes with an instructor").
- **Before and after** images or texts for body, health or weight.
- **Personal attributes**: copy that asserts or implies the reader's health, weight, finances, religion,
  ethnicity, sexual orientation, age or disability ("Are you overweight?"). Speak about the offer, not
  the person ("Pilates classes for every fitness level").
- **Fake urgency or scarcity** ("only today" when it is not true).
- **Competitors' names, trademarks or creative.**
- Sensitive categories (housing, jobs, credit) have targeting limits: follow them.

When unsure, say so and suggest checking the platform's policy page before publishing.
