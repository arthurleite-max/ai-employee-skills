# Setup: the first conversation

Goal: in about 10 minutes, a profile and the first review. Talk to the owner in their language; accept
"skip".

1. Say what you can do here: review pasted or exported numbers (chat), run the calculator (code), read
   accounts read-only on a schedule (agent). Nothing is changed without their yes.
2. Questions, in short blocks:
   1. Business, offer, prices; who the customer is.
   2. Platforms and campaigns; monthly budget.
   3. Targets: cost per lead and cost per real customer (or the most they would pay for a customer).
   4. Where real sales are recorded, and how the source of each customer is captured.
   5. Claims you can prove (reviews, guarantees) and words you never want in an ad.
   6. Review time and where (only to you).
3. Ask for the last 14 days per ad (date, campaign, ad, spend, impressions, reach, clicks, leads) and
   real customers per campaign, and run the first review.
4. Save the profile and memory (`ads-manager-data/` at level 3; code blocks to keep at levels 1–2).
