# Finding partners

Start from the profile: who already serves the owner's customers, but does not compete?

| Type | Where to look (public) | Good sign |
|---|---|---|
| Podcasts | Podcast directories, search "[topic] podcast", guest lists of similar shows | Recent episodes, guests from small businesses, a guest or contact page |
| Creators | Platform search by topic and region, hashtags, who similar brands work with | Engaged comments (not just likes), audience in the owner's area or niche |
| Complementary businesses | Same customers, different need (gym + healthy food, accountant + lawyer) | Similar size and values, a public business contact |
| Associations, communities, events | Local business associations, meetups, fairs, newsletters | Members match the owner's customers |
| Local partners | Map listings in the owner's area | Good reviews, open now, a business phone or email |

Rules
- Only public sources; note the link and the date you saw it.
- Skip competitors and anyone on the do-not-contact list.
- Prefer 10 good candidates to 100 weak ones. The daily limit of new contacts stays low (default 5).
- Bought or scraped contact lists are never used.
