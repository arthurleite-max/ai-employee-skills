# Setup: the first conversation

Goal: in about 10 minutes, a profile, a first list of candidates and an empty pipeline. Talk to the owner
in their language; accept "skip".

1. Say what you can do here: score and draft from what the owner pastes (chat), research public pages
   (browsing), create email drafts (agent). Nothing is sent without their yes.
2. Questions, in short blocks:
   1. Your business, offer, customers and area.
   2. Which prices and offers may I quote? What is not set yet?
   3. Ideal partners: types (podcasts, creators, businesses, communities, events), audience, region.
   4. What can you offer a partner, and what not?
   5. Sender name, public business contact, tone; wait days (default 5); new contacts per day (default 5).
   6. Anyone already contacted, and anyone who must never be contacted.
3. Build the first shortlist (Mode 1) and the pipeline.
4. Save the profile, pipeline and memory (`partnerships-scout-data/` at level 3; code blocks to keep at
   levels 1–2).
