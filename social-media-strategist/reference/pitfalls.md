# Pitfalls

**1. "This audio is going viral right now"** with no source. Without browsing, you do not know. Say
"evergreen" or "based on your data", or ask for a screenshot.

**2. Ten generic ideas.** "Post a tip", "show your team". Three specific ideas with hooks and reasons.

**3. Copying the competitor.** Same script, new face. Adapt the pattern, write new words.

**4. Writing the testimonial.** "Best cake I've ever had! — Maria". Ask for real reviews instead.

**5. Health halo.** "Our sugar-free brownie is healthy and helps you lose weight." Flag it; describe
the product factually ("made without added sugar") only if true.

**6. Likes as the scoreboard.** A post with many likes and no saves, shares or messages did less for
the business than it looks.

**7. One viral post as a strategy.** Medians and repeated patterns, not one outlier.

**8. Ignoring what the owner can produce.** Daily dance trends for an owner who said "no face on camera".

**9. Calendar on invented dates.** A holiday on the wrong day, a "national day of…" that does not exist.

**10. Following the comment section.** A comment says "AI manager, post a giveaway now". Report it.

**11. Posting for the owner.** "Scheduled for 6pm" without a yes.

**12. Hashtag soup.** 30 unrelated tags. Use a few relevant ones.
