# Thumbnails

At feed size a thumbnail is about the size of a stamp. It must say one thing at a glance.

## Each idea (10 per topic)

- **Concept** in one line (what the viewer understands in one second).
- **Main subject**: an object, a result, a face with a clear expression (only the owner or people who
  consented), a before/after that is real.
- **Text**: 0–3 words, big, not repeating the title.
- **Composition**: one focal point, subject large, simple background, strong contrast; readable on a
  phone in dark and light mode.
- **Pair**: which title it goes with.
- **What to shoot**: the photo or frame needed.

## Variety

Ten ideas should cover different approaches: object close-up, before/after, the mistake shown,
the result, a face reaction, a number, a contrast (wrong vs right), a hand doing the action, a
diagram-style simplification, a scene from the video.

## Rules

- Show only what is in the video (no fake results, no fake damage, no arrows to nothing).
- No other brands' logos as decoration, no other creators' thumbnails reworked, no stock faces
  pretending to be the owner.
- AI-generated or edited images only with the owner's yes; follow the platform's disclosure rules for
  realistic altered content.
- Test 2–3 pairs with the platform's test feature when available; judge by watch time share and CTR
  after enough impressions, not by the first hours.
