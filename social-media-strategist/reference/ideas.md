# Ideas

## Pillars (default mix; the profile can change it)

| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Educate | ~40% | Mistakes to avoid, how-to, myths, "what I'd do if…", answers to real customer questions |
| Behind the scenes / people | ~25% | How the product is made, a day in the shop, the team, the process, the tools |
| Proof and community | ~20% | Real reviews (with permission), customer stories, results the owner can show, events, local partners |
| Offer | ~15% | Products, services, launches, real promotions and deadlines |

## Formats per platform (keep it producible)

| Platform | Strong formats for small businesses | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | 15–45 s talking head with B-roll, process videos, before/after of the work (real) | Hook in 1–2 s; on-screen text; vertical |
| Instagram carousel | 5–10 slides: list, step-by-step, myth vs fact | Slide 1 is the hook; last slide the CTA; saves |
| Instagram Stories | Polls, questions, behind the scenes, offers | Daily, low effort |
| TikTok | Native, unpolished, story or reaction, series | Trending sounds from the app's library |
| YouTube Shorts | Same as reels, searchable titles | Reuse with a new title |

Only suggest formats the profile says the owner can produce. A business that never films faces gets
process videos, voice-over and carousels.

## Idea patterns that work for local and small businesses

- **The question you hear every week**, answered in 30 seconds.
- **The mistake**: "3 things people get wrong about [topic]".
- **The process**: from raw material to finished product.
- **The number**: a real number from the business (orders today, years, minutes it takes) — only real.
- **The comparison**: option A vs option B, with honest pros and cons (no competitor names).
- **The story**: a real customer situation, told with permission and without personal data.
- **The series**: "Monday tip", "behind the oven Fridays" — easy to repeat.
- **The seasonal moment**: dates that matter to the audience (only real, from the calendar or owner).

## A good idea has

A clear audience (who stops scrolling), one point, a reason to save/share or message, a format the
owner can make today, and a link to the business without being an ad every time.
