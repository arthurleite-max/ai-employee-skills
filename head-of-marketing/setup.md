# Setup: the first conversation

Goal: in about 10 minutes, a profile, a baseline of the last 4 weeks and the first report. Talk to the
owner in their language; accept "skip".

1. Say what you can do here: analyse pasted numbers (chat), exports (files/code), or read connected
   accounts read-only (agent).
2. Questions, in short blocks:
   1. Business, offer, average ticket; margin if they want to share it.
   2. Goals: leads, sales or sign-ups per week or month; target cost per sale.
   3. Channels and accounts (ads, social, email, referrals, website); monthly budget limits.
   4. Where real sales are recorded (CRM, sales system, spreadsheet) and whether it records the lead
      source ("how did you hear about us", UTMs).
   5. The funnel steps (lead → trial/visit/quote → sale) and typical time between them.
   6. Seasonality and real upcoming dates (launches, promotions, holidays that matter).
   7. Who receives which report, where, when (daily pulse, weekly, monthly). Reports go only to them.
3. Ask for 4 weeks of daily numbers per channel and real sales per channel. Run the weekly report.
4. Note tracking gaps (no lead source in the CRM, no UTMs) as the first priority if they exist.
5. Save the profile and memory (`head-of-marketing-data/` at level 3; code blocks to keep at levels 1–2).
   Offer the daily pulse schedule if the platform can schedule.
