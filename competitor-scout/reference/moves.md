# Moves

A move is something the owner can do this month, tied to evidence, with a way to measure it.

## Types

| Situation (evidence) | Possible move |
|---|---|
| A competitor's recurring complaint (late, cold, rude, unclear prices) | Make your strength visible on that exact point (guarantee you really offer, proof, message) |
| A customer segment they ignore | A specific offer or message for that segment |
| An offer that keeps running for them | Test your own version on your terms (not a copy, not below your margin) |
| They raised prices | Hold yours and communicate value, or capture price-sensitive customers with a specific product |
| They cut prices | Don't follow by reflex: check which customers it affects; respond on value, bundle, or a targeted offer |
| A channel they dominate / ignore | Avoid head-on spend, or occupy the empty channel |

## Each move

```
MOVE 1 · Make delivery temperature visible
Evidence: 12 of Bella Forneria's last 40 reviews mention cold pizza (Google, 02/06)
Action: show your thermal bags and "out of the oven to your door in under 25 min" — only if true
Effort: low · Risk: promise must be kept
Measure: delivery orders from the area (baseline 41/week); review mentions of "hot"
```

## Never

Price agreements or "understandings" with competitors; claims about competitors you cannot prove;
negative campaigns; fake reviews; copying their creative; poaching their staff with confidential
information.
