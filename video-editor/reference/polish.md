# Polish: B-roll, graphics, sound, music, colour

Polish happens **after** the cut is approved. Everything here is placed on the final timeline.

## B-roll

- Only files the owner provided or has rights to. List what you used and from where.
- Cover a line that **describes something visible** (a product, a place, an action), or a jump cut
  that looks bad. Do not cover the speaker's face during the hook or an emotional line.
- 1.5 to 4 seconds per shot in short-form; cut on the spoken beat.
- If no B-roll exists, say which shots would help most (a shopping list for the next recording).
- AI-generated or stock footage only with the owner's yes, and say which shots are generated.

## Graphics

- **Lower third:** name and title from the profile, once, early.
- **Key text:** only words that were spoken (the number, the step name). Never a new claim.
- **CTA card:** the spoken CTA or the profile's standard CTA, if the speaker said one.
- Brand colours and fonts from the profile; if none, one font, white text, a single accent colour.
- Keep text inside the safe area and on screen long enough to read (about 3 words per second).

## Sound effects

Sparse and meaningful: a list item appearing, a transition between sections, the payoff. Never under
speech at a level that competes. Only from the owner's or a licensed library.

## Music

- Mood and tempo that fit the speaker, never louder than speech (about −18 to −22 dB under the voice,
  ducked when the speaker talks).
- **Only licensed music in the file**: the owner's library or royalty-free tracks they have a licence
  for. Trending commercial songs are added by the owner inside the platform at posting time (the
  platform licenses them there); the edit leaves room and says so.
- Fade in/out; end on a musical phrase when possible.

## Audio clean-up

At level 3: normalise speech to about −14 LUFS for social platforms (−16 for podcasts), true peak
below −1 dB; light noise reduction only if it does not make the voice robotic. Report the measured
numbers; you cannot listen, so do not claim it "sounds great".

## Colour and framing

- Light correction only (exposure, white balance) unless the owner asks for a look.
- Vertical from horizontal: crop around the speaker's face; never cut off the top of the head in a
  close-up; check that the subject stays inside the frame for the whole segment.
