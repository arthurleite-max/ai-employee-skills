# Copy

## The 5-second test

From the first screen a stranger should know: **what** the business does, **for whom**, **where** (if
local), **why this one**, and **what to do next**. If any is missing, the headline or the hero is the
first fix.

## Headlines

| Weak | Why | Strong (pattern) |
|---|---|---|
| "Welcome to our website" | Says nothing | "[Service] in [place] for [audience]" |
| "Excellence and quality since 2009" | Any business could say it | "[Outcome] without [pain]" |
| "Innovative solutions" | Jargon | "[Specific result] in [specific time]" — only if true |
| "The best clinic in town" | Unprovable | "[Real proof]: 4.8 stars from 127 Google reviews" |

Use the customer's words (from reviews, questions, messages) more than the company's.

## Offers and CTAs

- One primary action per page, said the same way everywhere: "Book an assessment", "Get a quote on
  WhatsApp", "Call now".
- Button text says what happens: not "Submit", "Click here", "Learn more" alone.
- Near the CTA: what happens next ("We reply within 1 business day") — only if true.

## Proof

Real and specific beats big and vague: review rating and count as stated in the profile, named
testimonials with permission, photos of the real place and team, certifications the owner holds.
Never create a testimonial, a rating, a client count or a logo wall. Missing proof → suggest how to
collect it (ask three clients, link the review profile).

## Voice

Follow the profile. Default: short sentences, "you" more than "we", plain words, no exclamation
marks in a row, no ALL CAPS sentences.

## Facts and placeholders

Every fact in new copy comes from the profile, the site or the owner. Otherwise:
`[PRICE — you confirm]`, `[DATE — you confirm]`, `[TESTIMONIAL — real client, with permission]`.
Superlatives and comparisons with competitors are flagged.

## Delivering rewrites

```
Home · hero headline
Before: "Bem-vindo ao nosso site"
After:  "Fisioterapia e pilates no Batel, com horário até as 21h"
Why:    says what, where and the main differentiator in one line (all facts from the profile)
```
