# Pitfalls

- **Label headlines** ("Market", "Team"): write the takeaway instead.
- **Wall of text**: split; notes carry the detail.
- **Charts that do not add up**: check parts of a whole and periods.
- **Truncated axes** that exaggerate growth on bar charts.
- **Unsourced statistics** copied from memory: mark `[source needed]`.
- **Borrowed images and logos**: rights first.
- **Testimonials with contact details**: remove them; ask permission.
- **No ask at the end**: close with the decision you want.
