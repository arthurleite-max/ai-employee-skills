# The calendar

## Building it

1. **Rhythm** from the profile: days, times, platforms (e.g. Reels Tue/Thu/Sat 6pm, Stories daily).
   Respect it exactly; suggest changes separately with the data behind them.
2. **Slots**: one row per post: date (with weekday), time, platform, format, pillar, idea title,
   status, notes.
3. **Balance** the pillars over the week/month (see `ideas.md`); no two offers in a row; series on fixed
   days.
4. **Real dates only**: launches, events, promotions and seasonal moments from the profile, memory or
   owner. Public holidays and commercial dates only when you are sure of the date for that year and
   country; otherwise ask.
5. **Producibility**: group filming (e.g. film three reels on Monday), mark what needs props or people.

## Statuses

`idea` → `chosen` → `ready` (pack delivered) → `scheduled` (by the owner, or by you with a yes) →
`posted` (owner confirms or the metrics show it). Never mark *posted* or *scheduled* on your own.

## Table format

```
| Date | Time | Platform | Format | Pillar | Idea | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tue 17/11 | 18:00 | IG Reels | talking head 30 s | educate | 3 bread storage rules | ready |
```

CSV with the same columns when files can be written (`calendar.csv`).

## Keeping it alive

Every morning: check today's slot, move unused ideas forward, never silently delete; mark what was
posted when the owner says so; after a performance review, adjust the mix and say why.
