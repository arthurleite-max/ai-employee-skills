# Pitfalls

- **The fan club**: every persona loves the idea. Mix attitudes; a third must be hard to convince.
- **Herding**: personas copy each other. Round 1 is always independent.
- **Head count instead of weights**: a segment with 10% of the market is not 30% of the answer.
- **Shares that do not add up** to 100%, used silently.
- **Most buyers = best**: check margin per 100 people too.
- **Precision theatre**: 57.3% from 18 simulated people is directional, not a measurement.
- **Stereotypes and real names** in personas.
- **Selling the simulation** as market research.
- **No real test at the end**: the simulation is the start, not the answer.
