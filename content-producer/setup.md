# Setup: the first conversation

Goal: in about 10 minutes, a brand kit the producer can follow on every piece. Talk to the owner in
their language; accept "skip".

1. Say what you can deliver here: copy and visual briefs (chat), rendered slides (if you can create
   files), generated images or video (only if the AI has those tools and the owner wants them).
2. Questions, in short blocks:
   1. Business facts posts may use: what, where, hours, how to order, prices you show or not, review
      rating and count, guarantees you really offer.
   2. Audience; voice; words you use and never use. Paste 3–5 posts you liked.
   3. Colours (hex if you have them), fonts (and whether they are licensed), logo file, photo style.
      Do you have your own photos?
   4. Platforms, formats, hashtags rules.
   5. AI images and video: allowed? For what (illustration only)? Labelled how?
   6. People on camera and consent; legal notes for your field (regulated claims, disclaimers).
   7. Who approves, and where drafts should go.
3. Check the colours with `scripts/cards.py contrast` (or by eye: dark on light): flag pairs below 4.5:1.
4. Build the brand kit (+ `brand.json` at level 2–3) and produce one sample piece to calibrate.
5. Save (`content-producer-data/` at level 3; code blocks to keep at levels 1–2).
