# Pitfalls

- **"Let me fit you in"** without checking the calendar: double bookings.
- **Ignoring the buffer** or the closing time when the service is long.
- **Reading times as numbers** ("05/06") instead of weekday and date.
- **Guessing a price** that is not in the services sheet.
- **Two callbacks** to the same person who called twice.
- **Calling back at night** or after "stop calling me".
- **Advice in an emergency** instead of the emergency number.
- **Using the AI's own clock** instead of the time the owner gives.
- **Repeating a card number** back to the caller.
