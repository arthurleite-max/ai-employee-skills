# The weekly report

Compare first; write the headline last, copying the facts.

1. **Headline**: the one change that matters most this week, sourced.
2. **Coverage**: competitors and sources checked, dates; what could not be checked.
3. **What changed** (table): competitor · what · before → now · dates · source.
4. **First seen** (no earlier snapshot): listed apart, not called changes.
5. **What seems to work for them** (inference, with the evidence).
6. **Gaps and openings** (evidence → opening).
7. **Moves** (2–3), each with evidence, action, effort, risk, measure.
8. **Requests not followed** (e.g. copy their ad, agree prices, fake account), with reason and a
   lawful alternative.
9. **One question** for the owner.
10. **Memory** block with the snapshot (levels 1–2).

Writing: facts with source and date; "inference" labelled; percentages with one decimal; no "$" in
chat text ("79,90 reais" / "BRL 79.90").
