# Sounds human, stays true

Before any text goes into the review pack, remove the patterns that make it read as machine-written.
Keep every fact, number, name and claim exactly; change only how it is said.

## Strongest tells (fix on sight)

| Tell | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Staged contrast | "It's not just bread — it's a ritual." | Say the fact: "We ferment it for 18 hours." |
| One-line closer that repeats the point | "Because quality matters." | Cut it. |
| Forced triads everywhere | "Fresh, local and delicious." | Keep the one that is true and specific. |
| Dashes everywhere | Three em dashes in a caption | Use full stops and commas. |
| Inflation | "A game-changer for your mornings" | "Toast it frozen; it's ready in 4 minutes." |
| Stock AI words | delve, elevate, unlock, seamless, journey, tapestry, game-changer, "in today's fast-paced world"; and their equivalents in other languages (e.g. Portuguese desvende, potencialize, jornada) | Plain words |
| Staged opener | "Have you ever wondered…?" | Start with the point |
| Emoji as bullets on every line | ✅ ✅ ✅ | Use them like the owner does, or not at all |
| Bold labels on every item | **Tip:** **Why:** | Plain sentences |

## Then

Read it aloud as the owner would. If a line needs a detail you do not have, ask for it or write a
simpler line; never invent the detail. `python scripts/cards.py lint caption.txt` flags common tells
(English and Portuguese) as a second check.
