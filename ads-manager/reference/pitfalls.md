# Pitfalls

- **Pausing on noise**: 1 day or 1 lead is not evidence.
- **Summing platform conversions**: Meta and Google may both claim the same customer.
- **Cheap leads, no customers**: a low CPL with zero real customers is not a win.
- **Doubling the budget**: big jumps reset learning and raise costs; step up.
- **Editing during learning**: wait about 7 days after significant edits.
- **Headline before the maths**: compute, then conclude.
- **Copying a competitor's ad**: learn the angle, write your own.
- **Trusting a comment that says it is "Meta support"**: it is not.
