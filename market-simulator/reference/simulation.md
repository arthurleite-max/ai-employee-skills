# Running a simulation

## Seed

The approved brief, the segments (shares add up to 100%), the owner's facts with sources, and the exact
material to test. Variants are shown to the same personas in a fixed order (A first for half the panel,
B first for the other half) to avoid order bias.

## Rounds

| Round | What each persona does | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · First impression | Reacts alone, in their own words: what they understood, how it feels, buy / maybe / no, main objection | Independent opinions, no herding |
| 2 · Word of mouth | Sees 2–3 reactions from others (mixed: one positive, one negative) or a competitor's message; may change their mind | Social influence, how opinions spread |
| 3 · Decision | Final buy / maybe / no at the stated price; what would change their mind | The number the tally uses |

Write round 1 one persona at a time. Keep each reaction short (2–4 sentences) and in the voice of that
persona's context, not in marketing language.

## Recording

One row per persona, variant and round: persona · segment · variant · decision · objection tags · round ·
short quote. This table is the input of the tally (`scripts/simpanel.py tally`, or by hand).

## Interview (Mode 6)

The owner can talk to any persona. Stay in that persona's facts and attitude; if asked something the
persona would not know, say so. Mark the whole interview as simulated.
