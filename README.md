# AI Employee skills

One skill per AI employee. Each one works with Claude, ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Gemini, coding agents or any AI chat, and answers in your language. Free plans work.

## How to install (1 minute)

1. Find your employee in the table below.
2. **On Claude:** click **download zip** (do not unzip it), then in Claude: Settings → Customize → Skills → **+ Add → Upload skill**.
3. **On ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Gemini or any other chat:** click **copy the text**, then the **Copy raw file** button at the top right of the file, paste it into your AI and send.

Each employee has its own **guide** with screenshots of every step.
