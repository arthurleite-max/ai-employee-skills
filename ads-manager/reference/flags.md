# Flags and what to propose

| Flag | Rule | Propose |
|---|---|---|
| Waste | spend ≥ 2 × target CPL and 0 leads, or 7+ days with CPL ≥ 2 × target | Pause the ad (approval) |
| Fatigue | CTR down 30% or more vs previous period and frequency above 3 | New creative or new audience; keep the offer |
| Scale | CPL ≤ 70% of target and the campaign is limited by budget, enough leads (≥ 10) | Budget +15–20%, re-check in 3–4 days |
| Expensive customers | cost per real customer above target while CPL looks fine | Check lead quality, form questions, follow-up speed |
| Tracking | spend with 0 leads after normal days, or CRM far from platform | Test the form/tag before judging the ad |
| Learning | campaign or ad edited in the last 7 days / in learning | Wait; no big edits |
| Policy risk | claims, "before and after", personal attributes, fake urgency | Rewrite (see `policies.md`) |

Thresholds are defaults; the owner may change them in the profile.
