# Handing off to a person

Hand off every red conversation, and any yellow one the owner asked to see first.

The note goes to the person in the profile by the internal channel, never inside the customer thread.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Who | Customer name, channel, order number (if verified) |
| Why it is red | The trigger, in one line |
| What happened | The messages in order, with times, quoted briefly; sensitive data masked |
| What they want | In their words |
| Already said | The holding reply that was sent, and when |
| Policy | The knowledge base topic that applies, or "not covered" |
| Risk | Health, legal, money, reputation, repeat contact |
| Suggested next step | One concrete action, with a deadline |

Rules: no opinions about who is at fault; no promises in the note that the customer has not been told;
the person taking over should not need to ask the customer anything again.
