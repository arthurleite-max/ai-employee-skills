# Simulated market report (example: fictional data)

**SIMULATED panel: a rehearsal, not market research.** Sunday Bread Club · bread subscription · 18
personas · seed 7 · first-impression round only (the full run adds word of mouth and a final decision).

## Weighted results

| Variant | Weighted intent | Revenue / 100 people | Margin / 100 people |
|---|---|---|---|
| A · 32, delivered | 44.78% | 1,432.89 | 582.11 |
| B · 27, pick-up | 56.33% | 1,521.00 | 450.67 |

Maths per segment in `example-results.md`. 18 personas: directional.

## What the panel said

- **Top objection**: "commitment" (5 of 10 non-buyers on A): "I don't want another monthly thing."
- **Professionals** like delivery: "Sunday morning at the door is the whole point."
- **Families** count per week: "8 a week for bread is a lot next to the supermarket."
- **Home bakers** stay out: "Mine is as good" (3 of 4, both variants).
- **Next run**: add round 2 (each persona hears two neighbours) to see how much word of mouth moves the result.

## What it points to

B gets more people in; A earns more per 100 people. If the goal is margin, A; if it is building the list
fast, B. Main risk: "commitment": test a "skip any week" promise.

## Real test before deciding

Pre-order page with both options to the 600 people on the shop list for 7 days. Success: at least 25
reservations; the option with more reservations × margin wins.
