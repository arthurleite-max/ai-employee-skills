The Front Desk is original text and code, inspired by ideas from open community projects. No files from
these projects were copied. Thanks to their authors:

| Project | License | Idea used |
|---|---|---|

| [twilio-samples/speech-assistant-openai-realtime-api-python](https://github.com/twilio-samples/speech-assistant-openai-realtime-api-python) | MIT | A minimal phone receptionist with a short system prompt and first message |
| [didww/ai](https://github.com/didww/ai) | MIT | Putting a voice agent on a real phone number |
| [whobot-com/openclaw-whobot-skill](https://github.com/whobot-com/openclaw-whobot-skill) | MIT | Choose, test and adjust a phone agent before and after going live |

Some projects without a stated licence were looked at for ideas only; nothing from them is included.

The folder follows the open Agent Skills format (`SKILL.md`), documented at
[agentskills.io](https://agentskills.io).
