# Conversion

The site's job is the action in the profile (call, WhatsApp, booking, quote, purchase). Check the path
to it on every important page.

## Contact paths

- On mobile, the primary contact is one tap: `tel:` link, WhatsApp link (`https://wa.me/<number>`),
  booking link. A phone number as plain text is a finding.
- The same number, address and hours everywhere (header, footer, contact page, map, schema).
- The primary CTA visible on the first screen and repeated after proof and at the end.

## Forms

- Ask only what is needed for the next step (name + phone or email + message is often enough).
  Sensitive data (ID numbers, health details) only if truly required, with a reason and a privacy
  notice.
- Labels on every field (placeholder text is not a label), clear errors, a confirmation message that
  says what happens next.
- A privacy notice near the submit button for any personal data (GDPR, LGPD, CCPA and similar).
- Never submit a live form to test it without the owner's yes.

## Trust near the action

Real review rating and count, a named testimonial, guarantees the owner actually offers, address and
photos for local businesses, secure-checkout signs only if true.

## Friction

Popups on entry, autoplay video with sound, carousels hiding the main offer, menus with 10+ items,
pages that need horizontal scrolling on a phone, prices hidden behind "contact us" when the owner is
happy to show them.

## Urgency

Only real: a dated promotion, a real number of places for a class. Never invented counters or "X
people are viewing this". Expired urgency ("ends Friday" two months later) is a finding.
