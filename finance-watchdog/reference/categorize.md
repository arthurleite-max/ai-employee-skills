# Categorising spending

Categories exist to answer "where does the money go and what changed", not to do bookkeeping. Keep them
few and stable so months compare.

## Business categories (default)

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Payroll and contractors | Salaries, freelancers, payroll taxes paid with payroll |
| Rent and occupancy | Rent, building fees, property tax |
| Utilities | Electricity, water, gas |
| Telecom and internet | Phone plans, internet, landlines |
| Suppliers and inventory | Raw materials, goods for resale, packaging |
| Software and subscriptions | SaaS, apps, cloud storage, music licences, memberships |
| Marketing | Ads, printing, agencies, platform fees for promotion |
| Transport and fuel | Fuel, tolls, ride-hailing, deliveries, parking |
| Professional services | Accountant, lawyer, consultants |
| Taxes and government | Business taxes, licences, government fees |
| Bank fees and interest | Account packages, transfer fees, card annual fees, overdraft and late interest, FX fees |
| Insurance | Business, vehicle, health plans paid by the company |
| Equipment and maintenance | Repairs, tools, equipment purchases |
| Meals and travel | Restaurants, hotels, flights |
| Personal (on a business account) | Anything the profile says is personal |
| Uncategorised | Anything you cannot place with evidence |

Households use: housing, groceries, restaurants, transport and fuel, utilities, telecom, health and
pharmacy, education and kids, insurance, subscriptions, shopping, entertainment, bank fees and interest,
loans, other. Around 13 categories keeps "other" small.

## Order of evidence

1. The memory's merchant map (the owner already confirmed it).
2. The profile's known suppliers and fixed costs.
3. The description itself, when it is unambiguous ("ELECTRICITY CO", "FUEL STATION").
4. An email receipt matching amount and date, when email is available.
5. Otherwise: **uncategorised**. Ask about the largest ones in one short block.

Never infer tax deductibility from a category.

## Merchant names

Card descriptions are messy: `PAY *XK 5521`, `PAYSRV*7731`, `SQ *CAFE`, `AMZN MKTP`. Group variants of
the same merchant only when the evidence is clear (same prefix and same amount pattern, or the owner
confirmed). Similar names alone do not prove it is the same merchant. Keep the original description in
the evidence.

## Business vs personal

The profile says which cards and accounts are business. A clearly personal item on a business card (gym,
streaming at home, personal shopping) is a finding, not an accusation: "GymOne 119.00/month on the
business card: personal? If so, move it to a personal card." Two reasons it matters: taxes and seeing
the real cost of the business.

## Report the quality

"Uncategorised: BRL 412 (1.3% of spending)". If it is above 10%, the categories are not reliable yet:
say so and ask about the top uncategorised merchants. Never force labels to hit a percentage.
