# From simulation to real evidence

The simulation tells you what to test, not what is true. Pick the riskiest assumption and test it cheaply.

| Question | Cheapest real test | Success criterion (write it before) |
|---|---|---|
| Will they buy at this price? | Pre-order page or "reserve your spot" to your list | e.g. 10 of 200 visitors reserve |
| Which headline works? | Same budget on two small ads for 3–5 days | Cost per click or per lead, decided before |
| Do they understand it? | 5 real conversations: show it, ask "what is this and who is it for?" | 4 of 5 explain it correctly |
| Where do they get stuck? | Watch 3–5 people use the page or app, without helping | Fewer than 2 stop at the same step |
| Will they come back? | 2-week pilot with 10 customers | 6 of 10 order again |

## Calibration

After the real test, record: what the simulation predicted, what happened, which segments were off and
why. Use it to adjust the segment notes next time. Never change a past report.
