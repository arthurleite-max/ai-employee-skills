# Setup: the first conversation

Goal: in about 10 minutes, a profile, a baseline of the owner's channel and a first list of niche
videos to study. Talk to the owner in their language; accept "skip".

1. Say what you can do here: analyse what they paste (chat), or also research live (browsing/agent).
2. Questions, in short blocks:
   1. Channel name and link, subscribers, language; the business behind it and what videos should bring.
   2. Audience and the problems they come with.
   3. Formats and rhythm (long-form, Shorts), what they can film, on camera or not.
   4. Voice; words and claims to avoid; safety topics that need "call a professional".
   5. Thumbnail style (face, object, colours, font) and what never to put on a thumbnail.
   6. 3–5 channels in the niche they watch or compete with (big and small).
3. Ask for data:
   - Own channel: YouTube Studio → Content → last 20 videos with views (and CTR, average view
     duration if they can), or a screenshot.
   - Niche: for each reference channel, the last 10–15 videos with views and dates (or let browsing
     collect them), plus the channel's subscriber count.
4. Run the first weekly report. Save the profile and memory (`youtube-data/` at level 3; code blocks to
   keep at levels 1–2).
