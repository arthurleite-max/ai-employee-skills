# Writing instructions that models follow

- **Testable, not vague.** "Never repeat a card number; write [card •••• 4444] at most" beats "be careful
  with sensitive data".
- **Define the tricky word.** "Late = still needs an answer AND nobody replied yet AND more than 30
  minutes" beats "flag late callbacks".
- **Order matters.** Number the steps; put tables and calculations before headlines; "write the summary
  last".
- **Show the maths.** Ask for the subtraction or the sum line by line; free chat models mis-add silently.
- **Give the clock.** "Now is the date and time the owner gives, never your own clock."
- **Concrete examples with real-looking values**, never placeholders that get copied literally
  ("[card •••• last 4]" will be printed as is).
- **Name the boundary.** "start + length ≤ closing time" catches what "within opening hours" misses.
- **Say who acts.** At level 1 the AI drafts; the owner sends. Never "I will send" without a connected tool.
- **Plain English.** Short sentences, common words, no jargon; the AI translates for the user.
- **Money.** Tell the model not to type currency symbols that some chat apps render as formulas; write
  "60 reais" or "BRL 60".
