# Outreach messages

## First message (under 120 words)
1. Subject: plain and specific ("Guest idea: seasonal produce on a budget").
2. Line 1: the verified hook ("Your episode on farmers' markets (12/05) …").
3. Line 2: who the owner is, in one line.
4. Lines 3–4: the idea and what is in it for them.
5. One easy question ("Would a 20-minute call next week work?").
6. Sender name and a public business contact.

## Follow-ups (at most 2)
- Wait the owner's days (default 5) after the last message.
- Add something new (a short idea, a relevant fact, a sample offer); never "just bumping this".
- Follow-up 2 says it is the last one and leaves the door open.

## Replies
- Answer exactly what they asked, using only facts in the profile.
- Price, terms, dates or commitments the owner has not set → placeholder `[owner to confirm]` and ask
  the owner.

## Never
- "I listened to / loved your episode" when you only verified that the episode exists: write "I saw
  your episode of [date] was about …" unless the owner said they listened.
- Fake familiarity, flattery you cannot back, invented results or audience numbers.
- Pressure, false deadlines, attachments or links the owner did not provide.
- Undisclosed paid promotion: sponsored content is labelled as advertising.
