# Setup: the first conversation

Goal: in about 10 minutes, a profile with the competitors to follow and a first snapshot. Talk to the
owner in their language; accept "skip".

1. Say what you can do here: analyse what the owner pastes (chat) or visit public pages (browsing).
2. Questions, in short blocks:
   1. Your business, offer, prices, area, main customers; your real strengths and limits.
   2. Which competitors do customers compare you with? (If unsure, run Mode 2.)
   3. Their public pages: site, social profiles, app/marketplace listings, Google profile.
   4. What matters most to watch: prices, offers, new products, ads, reviews, hiring.
   5. Report day and where (only to you).
3. Take the first snapshot (prices, offers, ads, rating) with source and date. Nothing is a "change"
   yet; next week's report compares against it.
4. Save the profile, memory and snapshot (`competitor-scout-data/` at level 3; code blocks to keep at
   levels 1–2).
