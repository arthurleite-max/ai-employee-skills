# Short video pieces

## Script

```
REEL · 25–30 s · goal: saves
0–2 s   HOOK   spoken + on screen (same words)
2–8 s   point 1 · shot: …
8–16 s  point 2 · shot: …
16–24 s point 3 · shot: …
24–28 s CTA
Cover: frame at 6 s + text "…" (≤ 5 words)
Captions: on (verbatim)
Sound: pick from the app's library, low under the voice
```

## Shot list

What to film, from which angle, how long, with what (props, product), and who appears (consent).
Group shots by location so the owner films everything in one go.

## AI-generated video (only with a yes)

One prompt per shot: subject, action, camera move, light, duration, aspect ratio, what must not
appear. Label as generated. Never: testimonials, the owner's real team or customers, the business's
real place shown as if filmed, product results. For a real business, real footage builds more trust;
suggest it first.

## Hand-off to editing

If the owner uses the Video Editor skill or an editor: the script, the shot list, the on-screen text
and the CTA are the brief.
