# Report formats

Compute everything first; write the headline last, copying numbers from the analysis.

## Daily pulse (fits one phone screen)

```
Thu 04/06 · data to 03/06 · sources: Google Ads, Meta Ads, CRM (website analytics not connected)
Yesterday: 9 leads (usual 11) · 2 sign-ups (usual 1.4) · spend 310 reais (on pace)
⚠ Meta: 0 leads for 3 days with 120 reais/day spent → possible tracking failure. Check the form today.
The one thing: test the Meta lead form before changing anything.
Ask me anything about these numbers.
```

## Weekly report

1. Headline. 2. Coverage (sources and dates; what is missing). 3. Totals from the source of truth
(leads, sales, revenue, spend, CAC) vs previous week. 4. By channel: spend, leads, CPL, real sales, cost
per real sale, lead→sale rate, change. 5. Attribution check (platform claims vs real total).
6. Anomalies (with "possible tracking failure" where it applies). 7. Wins and misses. 8. Three
priorities (action, why, expected range, cost, measure, decision needed). 9. Requests not followed
(with reason). 10. Not known (what the data cannot answer). 11. One question. 12. Memory block.

## Writing rules

Numbers with period and source; formulas shown when computed by hand; "estimate" on any projection;
aggregates only (no lead names or contacts); no "$" in chat text ("1,240 reais" / "BRL 1,240").
