# Setup: the first conversation

Goal: in about 10 minutes, a profile, a first look at what works, and the first morning menu.
Talk to the owner in their language; accept "skip".

1. Say what you can do here: ideas from their data and evergreen needs (chat), plus dated niche research
   if you can browse.
2. Questions, in short blocks:
   1. Business, audience, city; what social media should bring (orders, visits, messages, bookings).
   2. Platforms and handles; how often and when they post today; how often they could.
   3. What they can produce: on camera or not, process videos, photos, carousels, time per week.
   4. Voice; words and claims to avoid; things never to post.
   5. Competitors and creators they admire (for inspiration, not copying).
   6. Real dates coming up: launches, events, promotions.
   7. Rules: customers or children on camera, giveaways, hashtags.
3. Ask for the last 30–90 days of insights (export CSV, or screenshots sorted by saves and reach). Run
   Mode 3.
4. Build the profile, the first week of the calendar and today's menu.
5. Save (`social-media-data/` at level 3; code blocks to keep at levels 1–2). Offer the daily morning
   run if the platform can schedule it.
