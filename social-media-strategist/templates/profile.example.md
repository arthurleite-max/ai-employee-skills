- Audience: families and young professionals within 5 km; event cakes for birthdays
- Goal of social media: orders via WhatsApp and visits on weekends
- Language of posts: Portuguese (Brazil)

## Platforms and rhythm
- Instagram @crumbandco.example: Reels Tue/Thu/Sat 18:00 · carousel Wed 12:00 · Stories daily
- TikTok: reuse reels on Sat
- Can produce: hands and process videos (no face on camera), photos, carousels; owner talks on
  camera only on Saturdays

## Pillars (adjusted)

## Voice
- Warm, curious, short sentences, one emoji at most, no ALL CAPS
- Avoid: "healthy", "guilt-free", "the best in town", prices in posts (they change)
