# Building the panel

## Size and spread

- 12–30 personas. Spread them by segment share with the largest-remainder method (the script does it):
  24 personas × 50% = 12; × 30% = 7.2 → 7; × 20% = 4.8 → 5.
- Shares must add up to 100%. If they add up to 110%, show the sum and ask; if you must continue, divide
  each by 110 and say so.

## Each persona

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Segment | Busy professionals |
| Need (job to be done) | Fresh bread without a trip to the bakery |
| Budget | About 30 a week for food extras |
| Current alternative | Supermarket bread; a meal-kit app |
| Attitude | Enthusiast · pragmatic · sceptic · price-sensitive · loyal to a competitor · undecided |
| One habit | Orders from the phone after 9 pm |

## Mix of attitudes

Every segment mixes attitudes. At least a third of the panel is sceptic, price-sensitive or loyal to a
competitor. A panel where everyone loves the idea is a broken panel.

## Never

- Stereotypes: no behaviour derived from ethnicity, religion, region, gender or age. Describe needs and
  context instead.
- Real people: no named influencers, clients, competitors' owners or public figures. Use archetypes.
- Invented facts presented as facts: what the owner did not give is an [assumption].
