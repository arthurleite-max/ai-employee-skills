# Design

- **Grid and margins**: same left margin and headline position on every slide.
- **Type**: headline 28–40 pt, body 18–22 pt, sources 10 pt. One font family (the brand's or a safe
  system font).
- **Colour**: background light or dark, text with strong contrast (4.5:1 or more), one accent colour for
  what matters. Avoid colour as the only signal.
- **Layouts**: title · bullets · big number · chart · two columns · quote · closing ask.
- **Images**: only the owner's own, licensed or free-licence (credited). No competitor material, no
  logos the owner may not use.
- **Whitespace** is fine; an empty half-slide beats a crowded one.
