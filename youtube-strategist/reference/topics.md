# Topics with room to win

A good topic for a small channel has **proven demand** (people watch it), **room** (the existing videos
are few, old, long or weak) and **fit** (the owner can make it well and it leads to the business).

## Score each candidate (with evidence)

| Criterion | Evidence | 0–2 |
|---|---|---|
| Demand | Outliers on 2+ channels, many views per day, questions in comments | |
| Room | Few videos on the exact problem, most older than 12–24 months, or long and unfocused | |
| Fit | The owner knows it, can film it, and it connects to what they sell | |
| Small-channel proof | A channel of similar size had an outlier on it | |

Total out of 8. Show the score **and** the videos behind it. A topic with no evidence is an idea, not a
recommendation: label it so.

## Angles that open room on a crowded topic

Narrower problem ("dripping single-handle tap" instead of "tap repair"), a constraint ("without
tools", "in 5 minutes", "without calling a plumber"), a mistake ("the mistake that makes it drip
again"), a comparison ("A vs B, which lasts"), local relevance (materials sold in the owner's region).

## Output

Three topics, each: topic and angle · score · evidence (2–3 videos with channel, views, ratio, date)
· why it fits this owner · risk (what could make it fail).
