# Market brief (example: fictional data)

- **Business**: Sunday Bread Club (sundaybread.example), a neighbourhood bakery
- **What we test**: a weekly bread subscription, delivered on Sundays
- **Variants**: A = 32 a month, 4 loaves, free delivery · B = 27 a month, 4 loaves, pick-up only
- **Cost per subscriber**: 19 a month
- **Decision**: which variant to launch first, or neither
- **Segments**: see `segments.example.csv` (shares from 6 months of card sales by postcode and time)
- **Alternatives today**: supermarket bread, our walk-in shop, a meal-kit app with bread add-on
- **What we know (sources)**: 41 reviews (average 4.6); top praise "crust"; top complaint "sold out by
  11 am"; 12 customers asked about delivery in the last 3 months (shop messages)
- **[assumptions]**: delivery matters more than price for professionals; families compare per-week cost
