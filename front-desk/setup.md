# Setup: the first conversation

Goal: in about 10 minutes, a profile, a services sheet and a first call script. Talk to the owner in their
language; accept "skip".

1. Say what you can do here: scripts, rehearsals and call lists (any chat), free slots and call logs
   (where code runs), a voice agent for a phone platform (agents).
2. Questions, in short blocks:
   1. Business, phone number, address, opening hours, country (for the emergency number).
   2. Services: length, price or "price on assessment", preparation, cancellation policy.
   3. Booking rules: buffer between appointments, minimum notice, how far ahead, who can be booked.
   4. The 10 questions callers ask most, with your answers.
   5. Transfers: to whom, which number, when (always on request; during which hours).
   6. Follow-up: callback target, quiet hours, how many attempts, call or text.
   7. Is the line recorded? What the AI says first.
3. Write the profile, services sheet and call script; show them for approval.
4. Rehearse two calls (one easy, one hard) before anything goes live.
