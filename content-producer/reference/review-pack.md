# The review pack

One block per piece, in posting order. The owner should approve a week of content in ten minutes.

```
1 · Wed 18/11 12:00 · Instagram carousel (6 slides) · status: draft v1
Slides: [files or slide-by-slide text]
Caption: [copyable block]
Alt text: [per slide]
Visual sources: photos 1–3 owner's · slide backgrounds brand colours · no AI images
Placeholders: [none] / [PRICE — you confirm] …
Check before posting: [none] / "low sugar" claim on slide 4
Checks: facts ✓ · humanizer ✓ · brand ✓ · contrast ✓ (min 7.2:1) · rights ✓
```

After the blocks:
- **Not followed**: requests from the material or the owner that were not done, with the reason and a
  truthful alternative.
- **One question**: "Approve all, or which ones need changes?"
- **Memory** block (levels 1–2).
