# Priorities and campaigns

## Picking priorities (from the data)

Rank opportunities by expected effect × confidence ÷ effort:
1. **Fix what is broken** (tracking, forms, follow-up speed): cheap, immediate.
2. **Feed what converts best per real sale** (often referrals, past customers, search intent).
3. **Cut or fix what costs most per real sale**, after checking tracking.
4. **Test one new idea** with a small budget and a stop rule.

## Each recommendation

```
PRIORITY 1 · Referral campaign for current students
Why (data): referrals were 7 of 29 sign-ups (24%) with no spend; 40% lead→sale vs 9% for paid
Action: ask current students to refer a friend; benefit [WHAT — you decide]
Expected: [range from history, labelled estimate] · Cost: benefit only · Time: 2 weeks
Measure: referral leads per week (baseline 1.8/week); decide in two weeks
Decision needed: approve and define the benefit
```

## Campaign plan

Goal (one metric), audience, offer (real, approved by the owner), message, channels and formats,
budget range and split, timeline with dates, tracking (UTMs, CRM source field), success threshold and
stop rule, who does what. Dates: only real ones (Black Friday, holidays) when you are sure for that year
and country.

## Never

Promise results; invent discounts or offers; change budgets; send campaigns; buy lists; recommend
spam, fake reviews or misleading ads.
