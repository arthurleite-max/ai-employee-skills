- Brightway Dental — family dental clinic, 2 chairs · average first treatment 900 reais (owner's figure)
- Goal: 20 new patients per month · target cost per new patient: up to 250 reais
- Monthly media budget: up to 4,000 reais

## Channels
- Google Ads (search) · Meta Ads (lead form) · Instagram organic · referrals · Google Business Profile
- Source of truth: the clinic's scheduling system, field "how did you find us"

## Funnel
- Lead → booked assessment → attended → treatment started (counted as a new patient)
- Typical time from lead to first visit: 3–7 days

## Calendar
- Slow in January and July; school holidays affect family bookings
- Real dates: anniversary campaign in March (owner confirms each year)

## Reports
- Daily pulse at 8:00 to the owner only (chat) · weekly on Monday · monthly on the 2nd
- Never share with the team without asking
