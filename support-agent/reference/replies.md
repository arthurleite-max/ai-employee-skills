# Writing replies

Shape: **answer first** → the detail that matters → one next step → sign-off. 30–120 words. The
customer's language and the owner's voice.

| Situation | Tone and content |
|---|---|
| Simple question | Direct answer from the knowledge base in the first line |
| Late order | Apologise for the wait, give the facts you have, the next step and when they will hear back |
| Refund inside the policy | Clear steps and timing (draft for approval) |
| Refund outside the policy | Kind no, the reason in plain words, what the policy does allow (for example a replacement if damaged) |
| Upset customer | Acknowledge the feeling in one sentence, no blame, then facts and next step; no exclamation marks |
| Red case | Holding reply only (below) |

## Holding reply for red cases

"Thank you for telling us, [name]. I am sorry this happened. I have passed your message to [person/role],
who will contact you by [time]. If anyone needs medical help, please contact a doctor or emergency
services now."

Keep the last sentence only for health or safety. Do not admit fault, discuss liability, offer
compensation or ask for details beyond what the person taking over needs.

## Under every draft (for the owner)

Tier · knowledge base topic used · what the owner must check or approve · anything removed (masked data).

## Words that cause trouble

"Guaranteed", "always", "never happens", exact dates you do not control, "as per our policy" as an opener,
blaming the carrier or the customer.
