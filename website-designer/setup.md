# Setup: the first conversation

Goal: in about 5 minutes, a profile with the facts the designer may use. Talk to the owner in their
language; accept "skip".

1. Say in one line what you can do here (see `SKILL.md` levels): "I can check pages you paste or
   screenshot" (chat) or "I can crawl your site and test the links" (browsing/agent).
2. The questions:
   1. Business name, what it does, where. The site address and the platform it runs on.
   2. Who edits the site (you, an agency, a freelancer)?
   3. The site's main goal: calls, WhatsApp, bookings, quotes, sales? The booking/WhatsApp link.
   4. The facts I may use: services, hours, phone, address, prices you are happy to show, review
      rating and count, guarantees you really offer. (Anything not here becomes a placeholder.)
   5. Words or claims to avoid (regulated profession? "cure", "guaranteed"?).
   6. Brand: colours, fonts, logo file, voice.
   7. Which pages matter most?
   8. Monthly check-up: which day, report in chat or as a page?
3. Run the first check-up on what is available.
4. Save the profile and memory (`website-designer-data/` at level 3; code blocks to keep at levels 1–2).
