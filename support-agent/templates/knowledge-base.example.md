# Knowledge base (example: fictional data)

Willow Tea House · online tea shop · willowtea.example
One "## Topic" per answer. Write the answer the way you want customers to read it. Add the date you last

## Opening hours and contact
Customer service: Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm. Messages after hours are answered the next working day.
Email help@willowtea.example · chat on the website. (checked 2026-05-02)

## Shipping times and costs
Orders placed before 1 pm ship the same working day. Delivery: city area 1–2 working days; rest of the
country 3–6 working days; islands and remote areas 7–10 working days. Shipping is free above 60; below that
it is 6.90. Tracking link is emailed when the parcel ships. (checked 2026-05-02)

## Returns and refunds
Unopened products can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund (customer pays return
shipping). Damaged or wrong items: we replace or refund within 30 days of delivery and pay the shipping; a
photo of the item is needed. Opened tea that is not damaged cannot be returned. Refunds go back to the
original payment method within 5 working days of receiving the return. Exceptions: only the owner decides.
(checked 2026-05-02)

## Order changes and cancellation
An order can be changed or cancelled until it ships (usually the same day before 1 pm). After shipping, use
the returns policy. (checked 2026-05-02)

## Payment methods
Card (Visa, Mastercard), bank transfer and gift cards. We never ask for card numbers, passwords or codes by
chat or email. (checked 2026-05-02)

## Tea subscription
Monthly box with 3 teas, 24 per month, free shipping. Pause or cancel any time before the 20th for the next
month's box, in "My account > Subscription". (checked 2026-05-02)

## Caffeine and allergies
Each tea page lists caffeine level and ingredients. Our blends are packed in a facility that also handles
nuts. We cannot give medical advice: anyone pregnant, on medication or with allergies should check with
their doctor. (checked 2026-05-02)

## Gift wrapping and messages
Gift wrapping costs 3 per order; a handwritten card is free. Prices are not shown on the packing slip when
gift wrapping is chosen. (checked 2026-05-02)
