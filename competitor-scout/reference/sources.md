# Sources

## Allowed (public)

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Offer, prices, menus, packages | Their website, public price lists, menus, marketplace and delivery-app listings |
| Promotions | Website banners, social posts, newsletters the owner legitimately subscribes to with their own email |
| Ads | Public ad libraries of the platforms (e.g. Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency Center, TikTok Creative Center) |
| Content and engagement | Public social profiles, YouTube, blog |
| Reputation | Public reviews (Google, marketplaces, review sites); their replies to reviews |
| Direction | Press, public announcements, job posts, new locations, permits that are public |

## Not allowed

- Logging into their customer areas, private groups or tools; using someone else's login.
- Fake accounts, fake identities, pretending to be a customer to extract internal information
  (costs, suppliers, client lists). A normal public quote request is fine only if the owner would
  honestly consider buying; never to obtain confidential terms.
- Scraping against a site's terms, bypassing paywalls or rate limits.
- Personal data about their staff or customers.
- Information obtained from the competitor's former employees under confidentiality.

## Recording

Each observation: competitor · item · value · source (link or "pasted by owner") · date seen. Keep the
screenshot or link when possible. Prices "from X" record exactly what the source says.

## Freshness

Weekly snapshots for prices and offers; ads checked weekly (note start dates the library shows);
reviews: count and rating weekly, themes monthly.
