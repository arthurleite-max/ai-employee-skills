# Research and angle

For each shortlisted partner, one short card:

- **Who**: what they do, for whom, size signals (with source and date).
- **Recent work**: 1–3 items with links and dates (an episode, a post, a launch).
- **What they might gain**: content for their audience, a product for their customers, a co-event,
  a commission, visibility. A value exchange, never a favour.
- **Best channel**: the public one they prefer (guest form, business email, booking page, official
  profile). Private numbers and personal addresses are out.
- **Angle**: one concrete idea (guest topic, bundle, workshop, joint offer).
- **Hook**: one verified fact for the first line, with its source. If you cannot verify a fact, do not
  use it.
- **Risks**: values mismatch, competitor ties, paid-only partners (budget question for the owner).
