# The knowledge base

The Support Agent is only as good as what the owner wrote down.

## Shape

- One `## Topic` per answer, named the way customers ask ("Shipping times and costs").
- The answer as the customer should read it, with numbers and conditions.
- The date it was last checked: `(checked 2026-05-02)`.
- Exceptions: say who decides ("only the owner decides").

## Where to start

Opening hours and contact · shipping · returns and refunds · cancellations · payment methods · product
care, sizes or ingredients · warranty · subscriptions · gift options. Then the owner's 10 most common
questions.

## Keeping it healthy

- Every gap (a question it could not answer) becomes a draft topic for the owner to approve.
- A resolved red or yellow case can become a topic, without names or personal data.
- Flag topics checked more than 6 months ago, and topics that contradict each other.
- Prices and dates change: the knowledge base wins over memory, and the owner wins over the knowledge base.
