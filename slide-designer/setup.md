# Setup: the first conversation

Goal: in about 5 minutes, a brief and a brand so the deck fits. Talk to the owner in their language;
accept "skip".

1. Say what you can do here: a slide-by-slide plan (any chat), an editable .pptx (where code runs).
2. Questions, in short blocks:
   1. What should the audience do after the presentation? Who are they?
   2. How long, in person or sent, and the deadline.
   3. The material: notes, documents, data (with sources), photos and logos you own.
   4. Brand: colours, font, tone; anything to avoid.
3. Propose the headline list (Mode 1) and wait for the owner's yes before writing every slide.
4. Save the brief and brand (deck folder at level 3; code blocks to keep at levels 1–2).
