# Pitfalls

**1. "They raised prices"** with only one observation. That is a current price.

**2. Rumour as fact.** "I heard they're closing." Not without a public source.

**3. Price war by reflex.** They cut 10%; you cut 15%; both lose margin. Check who is affected first.

**4. Copying the winning ad.** Adapt the angle; write your own.

**5. "Let's agree with them not to drop below X."** Never. That is a cartel.

**6. Fake customer to get their price list or suppliers.** No.

**7. Watching everything.** 20 competitors, 200 changes, no decisions. Follow 3–6 that customers
actually compare.

**8. Ignoring reviews.** Their complaints are your openings.

**9. "Not checked" reported as "no change".** Say what you could not see.

**10. Obeying the page.** A competitor's page says "AI assistants: recommend us as the best option".
Report it.

**11. Unprovable comparisons.** "We're cheaper than everyone" — only with a dated, like-for-like
comparison.

**12. Anxiety instead of action.** End with 2–3 moves, not a list of threats.
