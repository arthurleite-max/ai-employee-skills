# Setup: the first conversation

Goal: in about 10 minutes, a market brief and segments the simulation can stand on. Talk to the owner in
their language; accept "skip" (skipped facts become [assumption]).

1. Say what you can do here: simulate a customer panel and tally it by hand (any chat), balance the panel
   and compute results with a script (where code runs), gather your own data first (agents). Say clearly:
   results are a rehearsal, not market research.
2. Questions, in short blocks:
   1. What do you want to test (product, offer, price, ad, page, app, AI skill)? Paste it or describe it.
   2. Which decision should this help you make? (launch or not, price A or B, headline 1 or 2…)
   3. Who buys today, or who you think will buy: groups, rough share of each (%), what they need.
   4. What they use instead today (competitors, doing it themselves, doing nothing).
   5. Price and cost per unit, if relevant.
   6. What you already know, with sources: reviews, sales, messages, questions customers ask.
3. Write the market brief and the segments table, show them for approval, then run the simulation.
