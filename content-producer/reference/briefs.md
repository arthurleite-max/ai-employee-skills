# Briefs

Every piece starts from a brief, even a five-line one. It prevents rework and invented facts.

```
PIECE: Wed 18/11 · Instagram carousel · 6 slides · 1080×1350
GOAL: saves (educate) · AUDIENCE: first-time home bakers
ONE MESSAGE: bread lasts longer in cloth than in plastic
CTA: "Save this for your next loaf"
FACTS ALLOWED: from brand kit (storage advice the owner gave), nothing else
NOT ALLOWED: health claims, prices
SOURCE: calendar idea "Bread storage: 3 rules" (approved 11/11)
```

## Fill it from

1. The approved idea (calendar, strategist, owner's message).
2. The brand kit (facts, voice, CTA, links, rules).
3. The owner, only for what is missing. Ask once, in a short list.

## Missing facts

Never fill a gap with something plausible. Use a visible placeholder in the piece and list it in the
review pack: `[PRICE — you confirm]`, `[DATE — you confirm]`, `[PHOTO — the real oven]`.
