# The morning review

Order (headline last):
1. **Coverage**: platforms, accounts and dates read; anything not exported = "not checked".
2. **Pacing**: month to date, projected month, budget. Over 105% projected → propose a cut; under 85% →
   say why (limited campaigns, paused ads).
3. **Campaigns**: spend · leads · CPL · real customers · cost per customer · vs target.
4. **Ads**: this 7 days vs previous 7 days: CTR, CPC, CPL, frequency.
5. **Flags** (see `flags.md`).
6. **Change list** for approval (numbered): what, why, expected effect, risk, check date.
7. **One question** and the headline (one line, written last).

Fits a phone screen: the review is short; detail goes in the report.
